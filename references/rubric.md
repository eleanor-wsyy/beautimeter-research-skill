# Fifteen properties — provisional evidence rubric

The names and order below match Studio's `src/app/data/properties.ts`. Cues paraphrase the theory of centers and the supplied Bin Jiang Beautimeter paper (pp. 3–4, 6–8); they are **our operational questions**, not the professor's original GPT instructions. First assess the whole's nested centers and coherence across and within scales, then score the properties. One visually strong feature cannot automatically satisfy several unrelated items.

For each image independently, use 0.00 for clear *visible* lack or contradiction, around 0.50 for mixed/partial support, and up to 1.00 only for strong, integrated evidence. Intermediate hundredths are allowed if justified, but two decimal places do not imply measurement precision. Mark `N/A` when a viewpoint cannot establish the property; do not convert missing evidence into zero. Do not award a historical-style bonus or punish a building merely for being modern.

| # | English | 中文（Studio） | Visible evidence question |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Levels of Scale | 尺度层级 | Do large, medium, and many smaller centers form a supporting hierarchy rather than repeated details at one scale? |
| 2 | Strong Centers | 强中心 | Are centers strengthened by surrounding centers, rather than merely placed on one axis or highlighted by contrast? |
| 3 | Thick Boundaries | 厚边界 | Do substantial transitional layers protect and connect important centers, beyond a thin outline or decorated frame? |
| 4 | Alternating Repetition | 交替重复 | Is there rhythm with meaningful variation and adaptation, not just mechanical duplication? |
| 5 | Positive Space | 正空间 | Are both forms and the spaces between them coherently shaped, rather than one being leftover empty area? |
| 6 | Good Shape | 良好形状 | Do parts form legible, mutually reinforcing wholes rather than isolated motifs? |
| 7 | Local Symmetries | 局部对称 | Are there fitting symmetries within multiple parts, beyond one global bilateral axis? |
| 8 | Deep Interlock and Ambiguity | 深度交织与模糊性 | Do adjacent regions meaningfully interpenetrate and share transitions, beyond perspective overlap? |
| 9 | Contrast | 对比 | Do differences of scale, enclosure, form, or light clarify centers, beyond photographic color or exposure? |
| 10 | Gradients | 渐变 | Are gradual changes of scale, density, enclosure, or spatial depth structurally meaningful? |
| 11 | Roughness | 粗糙性 | Does fitting irregularity reveal adaptation, rather than damage, age, or image noise? |
| 12 | Echoes | 共鸣 | Do related shapes and centers answer one another across different parts and scales, beyond identical copied ornament? |
| 13 | The Void | 虚空 | Is a quiet open center formed and supported by its surroundings, not merely blank sky or wall? |
| 14 | Simplicity and Inner Calm | 简洁与内在平静 | Does complexity resolve into coherent order rather than mere visual minimalism? |
| 15 | Not-Separateness | 非分离性 | Do the visible parts, spaces, and context belong together rather than read as detached objects? |

**Comparison limits:** A cropped tower and a wide site photograph reveal different amounts of context. Do not penalize unseen ground-level relationships as if they were visibly absent; mark the affected property `N/A` if it cannot be assessed. Record which claim is about the *photograph* and which would require more views of the actual building. The total is an indicative sum, not a calibrated psychometric measure. The paper itself discusses mismatches with some reference pair judgments and the need to study interactions among properties; it does not specify a unique set of numeric thresholds or property weights.
